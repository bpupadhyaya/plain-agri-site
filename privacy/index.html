Privacy

PlainAgri is designed to know as little about you as possible.

The app

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • Your location is used on your device to find your nearest reference place and is not sent to us.
  • Your profile, saved plans and bookmarks stay on your device.
  • The only network use planned for the first release is opening a source when you tap a citation, and your store handling subscriptions.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, trackers or third-party fonts or scripts.

Subscriptions

Payments are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your payment details.
