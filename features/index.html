Features

A plan, and the tools to carry it out.

These screens are from the first-release build on iPhone and Android. It is not in the stores yet. Free shows you enough to judge it; Pro opens the whole library.

Try it in your browser Free and Pro

A library you can search

Free: index and searchPro: full guides

Learn is one hub for Guides, Crops, Diagnose, Calculators, Equipment and Glossary, with a single search box across all of them. Plan and Saved link straight into it.

515 places, 69 crops, 188 documents and 306 cited sources are in the library today.

Crop pages for your place

Free: crop factsPro: growing guide

Pick a crop to see when it can be sown and harvested where you are, with spacing, seed rate and water need clearly labelled as estimates. The growing guide and an Add to my plot button are in Pro.

Diagnose what you see

Free: 3 walk-throughsPro: all 38

Answer a few plain questions about the leaves, the fruit or the stored grain. You get likely or possible causes, what to check, what to do and what to avoid, each with sources. Results are never presented as a certainty, and chemical advice always points you to labels and local rules.

Walk a real diagnosis

Calculators with the formula showing

Free: rainwater, when to sowPro: all 9

Size a rainwater tank, work out how much irrigation water a bed needs, count plants and seed, plan fertilizer from a nutrient target, balance a compost heap, estimate storage space, and find your latest sowing date. Every calculator states its formula and assumptions and cites its sources.

Try two of them

My Plots and a season task list

Pro

Save each plot with its place, area, scale, water and crops. The app builds a season task list: prepare the soil, sow, harvest, with the calendar shifted correctly for your hemisphere and the equator. Reminders are optional, switched on per plot, and scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

This month, at the top

Free: outlinePro: your plots

Open the Plan tab and the first card says what to sow now, what to harvest now and what is coming next month for your place. With plots saved, your own open tasks join it.

Words explained, right where you read them

Free

234 terms in plain language. In plan and guide text, an underlined word opens a short definition with related terms and sources, then closes back to exactly where you were.

Equipment from a dibber to a drone

Free: the list

108 tools and machines across all seven scales: hand tools, draft animals, small and large machinery, robotic and precision systems, and aerial work. Filter by scale and kind, and every entry says what it needs, the area it suits and the manual alternative.

Take your plan with you

Pro

Export a plan, or one plot's plan, as a PDF with every section and a numbered source list with web addresses, and share it with the system share sheet. Handy for a co-op meeting, an extension visit or a landlord.

The PDF is plain and printable: your place and setup, each plan section, and a numbered list of sources.

Text size that fits you

Free, everywhere

Every screen has an A- and A+ control with the exact percentage, from 50 to 300 percent. It scales the whole app, including the tab bar and sheets, on phones, tablets and foldables, and layouts reflow instead of cutting words off. Try the zoom.

Your colours, NutriSize by default

Free, everywhere

The app opens in the clean NutriSize Health look: white and soft green with deep green and blue accents, always light. If you prefer something else, Appearance in the About tab offers Field (warm earth tones) and Night (dark). Your choice is saved on your device.

Fluid on every device

Free

Layouts adapt from small phones to tablets: reading width is capped and centred on wide screens, bars tuck away while you scroll at large text sizes, and the app rotates on iPad and Android. Checked on a range of simulated phones, tablets and a foldable.

Screens show sample places and plots. Guides are AI-drafted and spot-checked; nothing is called expert-reviewed until a named expert signs off. Always confirm local conditions and product labels.
