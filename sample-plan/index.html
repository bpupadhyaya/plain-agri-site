1. Site and soil

Choose the flattest, best-drained part of the plot, away from low spots where water pools. Check the soil by squeezing a moist handful: a ball that holds and then crumbles is a good sign; one that stays sticky or will not hold together needs different handling.

Typical for the region: highland soils are often well-drained but can be low in organic matter. Estimate; test your own soil.


2. What to grow here

This highland climate suits maize, beans, leafy greens such as kale, and potatoes. Mixing a cereal with a legume on the same plot is a long-standing way to spread risk and help soil fertility.


3. Calendar

Rainfall in this region is typically in two seasons: a longer one in roughly March to May and a shorter one in roughly October to December.
