Privacy

PlainAgri is designed to know as little about you as possible.

The app

  • No accounts and no sign-in.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • Your location is used on your device to find your nearest reference place and is not sent to us.
  • Your profile, saved plans and bookmarks stay on your device.
  • The only other network use is opening a source when you tap a citation, and your store handling subscriptions and purchases. Advertising is described below.

Advertising

The free version of the app shows advertising served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may use your device's advertising identifier (the IDFA on iOS or the Advertising ID on Android) and device and usage information such as your IP address, device type and how you interact with ads.

Where required (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before showing ads. You can decline tracking or personalisation, reset your advertising ID, or opt out in your device settings (iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking; Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads).

A one-time Remove ads purchase removes ads. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google. Learn more in How Google uses data in advertising.

This website

This site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, trackers or third-party fonts or scripts.

Subscriptions

Payments for subscriptions and the Remove ads purchase are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your payment details.
