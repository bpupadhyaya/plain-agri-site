About

One engineer, one mission.

PlainAgri is built by a highly experienced engineer, amplified by the world's most advanced AI agents: the craftsmanship of a dedicated expert with the capacity of a world-class engineering team.

The mission is simple: anyone, anywhere, should be able to plan what to grow from the resources around them, in plain language, with sources they can check. PlainAgri is part of the Plain family of apps from EqualInformation.

Content is cited, and nothing is described as expert-reviewed until a named expert has signed off.
