Three steps, no jargon

1

Say where

Use your phone's location, search for a place, or pick one from a list. Planning a plot you are not standing on? That works too.

2

Say what for

Food security, a garden, a small farm or a commercial operation, and the scale you work at. Every answer is optional and has a sensible default for your region.

3

Follow the plan

Site and soil, what to grow, a hemisphere-aware calendar, planting, water, fertility, pests, harvest, storage, and a manual fallback at every step.

From one plant to a whole landscape

Agriculture is whatever grows food, fibre or soil, at whatever scale. PlainAgri is being built to cover the whole range, so the plan matches how you actually work.

  • Hand Single plants, pots, raised bedsPlanting, watering and harvesting by hand.
  • Hand tools Small plots and family gardensHoes, dibbers, watering cans, hand seeders and simple drip kits.
  • Animal draft Working with draft animalsPloughing, ridging and hauling where fuel and machines are scarce.
  • Small machinery Walk-behind and compact tractorsTillers, small seeders, pumps and sprinklers.
  • Large machinery Full-size tractors and combinesSeed drills, planters, sprayers, harvesters and large irrigation systems.
  • Robotic and precision Guided, sensor-driven farmingGPS-guided machines, robotic weeding and variable-rate application.
  • Aerial Seeding and spraying from the airLarge-area aerial seeding and drone-based work, with safety and regulation in mind.

Any climate, anywhere

Tropical rainforest to cold continental winters, arid deserts to mountain gardens. Your place resolves to its climate zone, and the advice changes with it.

Cited, and one tap from the source

Every cited claim carries a small source chip. Tap it and the source opens inside the app in a sheet covering 80 percent of the screen, with Back, Forward and Close, so you always return to exactly where you were. Try the demo:

Soils rich in organic matter generally hold more water and drain better than depleted soils.

Demo only. In the app the chip opens the real source.

Private and offline-first

  • The baseline library and your plan are designed to work with no signal, in the field.
  • No accounts, no analytics, no ad trackers. Your location stays on your device.
  • Where something is a regional estimate (for example which fertilizers are typical near you), the app says so plainly.
